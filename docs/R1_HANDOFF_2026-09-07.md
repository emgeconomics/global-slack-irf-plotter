The framing is deliberately qualified: these references establish the

recoverable by simple OLS. The paper’s contribution is to expose the
model-implied transmission channels, cross-equation restrictions, and
measurement/specification problems that can make those effects difficult to
identify in reduced-form regressions.

## Canonical Euler-wedge names

The executable model, simulation code, tests, user interface, exports, and
SMM configuration now use only:

```text
zeta
zetastar
eps_zeta
eps_zetastar
deltazeta
deltazetastar
stdzeta
stdzetastar
corrzetazetastar
```

The retired `d`/`dstar` compatibility aliases are prohibited and are checked
by the migration/validation workflow.

The wedges are measured in real-rate-equivalent units and enter before the
open-economy aggregate-demand mapping. A private financing premium
`premium_t` maps to the maintained specification as `zeta_t = -premium_t`
when the conventional or maintained natural-rate measure omits that premium.
Thus, a rise in the private financing premium is a contractionary negative
`zeta` innovation even when the measured real-rate gap is unchanged.

A preference/discount-factor shifter is one possible microfoundation, but the
wedge can also summarize risk premia, credit spreads, borrowing constraints,
financial conditions, residual private demand, or fiscal-demand movements not
modeled separately. The benchmark continues to hold the productivity-driven
potential-output and natural-rate block fixed.
